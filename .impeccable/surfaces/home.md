## Mode

Experience

## Approved composition

`.impeccable/mocks/couture-curtain.png`

The user supplied a precise mobile-only visual direction. Browser-based and structured approval channels were unavailable, so the choice was delegated to the brief. The couture-curtain composition was selected because it best translates the dress inspiration, keeps jade dominant, and gives the opening a memorable interaction without competing with the event information.

## Direction contract

- World: a luminous jade couture gala based on the supplied dress photograph, built from deep green silk, emerald reflections, fabric flowers, silver embroidery, pearls, and restrained dusty-rose accents.
- First viewport: a restrained edge-to-edge entry layer with a custom KP · XV monogram, a short atmospheric phrase, and one clear opening control. Karen's name and date are deliberately withheld until the guest opens the invitation.
- Visitor path: open the curtain, reveal Karen's full name and date as the first document viewport, then use the phone's native scroll through the family, personal dedication, date, live countdown, connected event route, dress code, gifts, and confirmation.
- Signature interaction: the minimal entry opens like fabric curtains and hands off to the authored name-and-date composition with a short crystal shimmer.
- Motion grammar: slow fabric-like easing for the opening; small scroll reveals for section rules and copy; reduced-motion users see all content immediately.
- Typography: high-contrast luxury serif for display and a clean sans serif for practical information.
- Color relationship: deep jade (#075449–#0F8770) owns the large atmospheric regions, brighter emerald reflects from the silk, forest green protects legibility without reading black, cold pearl silver carries text and light sections, and pink appears only in tiny floral details.
- Family composition: parents and godparents are arranged as an offset editorial lineage joined by a silver thread and crystal points, never as plain centered stacks or cards.
- Personal signature: the custom KP · XV seal and a short dedication provide identity without using photographs; the seal returns only at the closing so it reads as a signature, not a repeated badge.
- Event utility: the ceremony and reception read as one connected afternoon route, with direct location actions and a universal calendar chooser: separate Google Calendar actions plus one interoperable file containing both confirmed times for Samsung, Apple, Outlook, and other calendar apps.
- Reserved colors: a full pearl-and-jade marble and crystal interlude carries the complete dress-code message above two realistic couture fabric swatches in Verde Jade and silver, echoing the supplied reference without baking browser chrome or text into the image.
- Gifts: the presence message becomes a self-contained calligraphic scene over the pearl marble world, anchored by a dimensional jade-and-silver gift box. Bank details live in a separate dark-jade “Un detalle especial” section so the transfer option does not visually merge with the reception coffer message; the account remains real, selectable, and functional.
- Scroll continuity: sections use intentional solid jade, forest, and pearl surfaces with crisp boundaries instead of gradients. Native touch scrolling replaces artificial interpolation; recurring paint-heavy blur, clip-path, and offscreen looping animations are avoided.
- Sound: the explicit “Abrir invitación” gesture starts the supplied song at a moderate volume; a persistent, accessible speaker control lets guests pause or resume it at any time, and the audio does not loop automatically.
- Honest risk: the curtain can become theatrical or template-like, so decoration stays asymmetric, the center remains quiet, and later sections use editorial rules rather than repeating the curtain motif.
