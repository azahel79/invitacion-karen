## Approved composition

The user supplied a precise mobile-only visual direction. Browser-based and structured approval channels were unavailable, so the choice was delegated to the brief. The couture-curtain composition was selected because it best translates the dress inspiration, keeps blue dominant, and gives the opening a memorable interaction without competing with the event information.

## Direction contract

- World: a luminous petroleum-turquoise couture gala based on the supplied dress photograph, built from saturated teal silk, cyan reflections, fabric flowers, silver embroidery, pearls, and restrained dusty-rose accents.
- First viewport: a restrained edge-to-edge entry layer with an XV crystal emblem, a short atmospheric phrase, and one clear opening control. Karen's name and date are deliberately withheld until the guest opens the invitation.
- Visitor path: open the curtain, reveal Karen's full name and date as the first document viewport, then scroll natively to meet the family, both venues, dress code, gifts, and confirmation.
- Signature interaction: the minimal entry opens like fabric curtains and hands off to the authored name-and-date composition with a short crystal shimmer.
- Motion grammar: slow fabric-like easing for the opening; small scroll reveals for section rules and copy; reduced-motion users see all content immediately.
- Typography: high-contrast luxury serif for display and a clean sans serif for practical information.
- Color relationship: clear dress-derived teal (#005262–#008FA6) owns the large atmospheric regions, brighter turquoise reflects from the silk, medium petroleum protects legibility without reading black, cold pearl silver carries text and light sections, and pink appears only in tiny floral details.
- Family composition: parents and godparents are arranged as an offset editorial lineage joined by a silver thread and crystal points, never as plain centered stacks or cards.
- Reserved colors: a full icy-blue marble and crystal interlude carries the complete dress-code message above two realistic couture fabric swatches in Azul Plumbago and silver, echoing the supplied reference without baking browser chrome or text into the image.
- Gifts: the presence message becomes a self-contained calligraphic scene over the icy marble world, anchored by a dimensional blue-and-silver gift box. Bank details live in a separate dark-teal “Un detalle especial” section so the transfer option does not visually merge with the reception coffer message; the account remains real, selectable, and functional.
- Scroll continuity: section surfaces remain painted at all times while only their inner content animates, preventing observer delays from exposing empty teal fields; content-led heights, material-aware color fades, and Lenis provide continuous motion while honoring reduced-motion preferences.
- Honest risk: the curtain can become theatrical or template-like, so decoration stays asymmetric, the center remains quiet, and later sections use editorial rules rather than repeating the curtain motif.
